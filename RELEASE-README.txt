SiliconShaders @VERSION@

Native Apple Metal renderer and shaders for Minecraft: Java Edition.

Requirements: Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer), macOS 15 or later, Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, Java 25.

Installation: put the SiliconShaders JAR in the mods folder of your Fabric instance. The settings are under
Options > Video Settings. Remove Sodium and Iris; they are not compatible.

If you used MCOpt before, replace its JAR and do not install both. Your MCOpt settings and profiles are imported
automatically; the original files stay where they are.

Bugs and crashes: https://github.com/rellefnediew/SiliconShaders/issues
